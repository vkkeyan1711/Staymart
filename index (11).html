Questions hotel owners ask

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code or open a link, and the form works in any phone’s browser.

How do I know the amount a guest enters is true?

The guest enters it themselves at check-in, and it can’t be changed from the dashboard. Compare it with the cash in your drawer and bank. Any gap shows up straight away.

Who can see the ID proofs?

Only someone with your dashboard PIN. Files open inside the dashboard, so staff don’t need Drive access or have to keep copies on their phones.

What if a guest has no smartphone?

Your receptionist can open the same form on the desk phone and fill it with the guest. The record still goes to your dashboard.

Do I need to buy any hardware or change my current software?

No. You need a phone and a printed QR code. StayMart works alongside whatever you already use.
